Personalised Teaching Meets Digital Resources

Since 2013, I've been helping professionals master English for their specific industries and goals. Based in Palma, Mallorca, I teach online via Zoom, focusing on business English and British culture for working professionals.

The resource library complements personalised one-on-one teaching sessions. While the modules provide structure and practice, our live sessions focus on your specific needs, industry vocabulary, and real-world application.

This hybrid approach means you're never wasting time in class on things you can learn independently, and you're never struggling alone with concepts that benefit from expert guidance.
